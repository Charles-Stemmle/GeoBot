Where was this street photo taken?

GeoBot predicts the country of a street-level image by comparing a general model with two specialists, one reading the road, the other the landscape, then combining their scores into a single ranking.

Method

Three views of the same place.

Each model contributes a different kind of evidence. The consensus combines their scores using configurable weights.

01Holistic

Analyzes the full image to learn broad patterns such as architecture, road layout, and the overall scene.

02Road specialist

Uses a segmented view focused on road-related features, including sidewalks, vehicles, poles, and signs.

03Environment specialist

Uses a segmented view emphasizing vegetation and terrain that may help distinguish regions.

About

Experiment, compare, improve.

GeoBot is an ongoing project investigating whether specialized visual features can improve country-level image classification.

Percentages are model scores, not real-world probabilities. Accuracy depends on the image, the training data, and how closely the photo resembles what the models learned from.
